Privacy Policy for 15ch JUKEBOX SAMPLER

Last updated: August 2026

15ch JUKEBOX SAMPLER ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it.

1. Information Collection and Use

The App operates entirely locally on your device. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, user identifier, usage analytics, or information from your selected local media folders (MP3/JPG files).

2. Local File System Access

The App requires read access to local folders selected explicitly by the user solely to read audio and image files for playback and visualization within the App. No local data is uploaded to external servers.

3. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at niirosoft.
